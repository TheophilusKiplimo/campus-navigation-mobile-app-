# Target Users

## Overview

The Campus Navigator Mobile Application is intended to help members of the university community and visitors easily locate buildings, offices, lecture halls, laboratories, and essential services.

## Primary Users

### 1. Students

Students use the application to locate lecture halls, laboratories, libraries, cafeterias, and administrative offices.

Needs:
- Find lecture venues quickly.
- Locate university services.
- Save frequently visited places.

---

### 2. Lecturers

Lecturers use the application to locate classrooms, departments, meeting rooms, and university offices.

Needs:
- Search teaching venues.
- Find departmental offices.
- Navigate efficiently across campus.

---

### 3. University Staff

Administrative and support staff use the application to locate offices and service departments.

Needs:
- Find internal offices.
- Locate service units.
- Share directions with visitors.

---

### 4. Visitors

Visitors such as parents, guests, and new applicants often have little knowledge of the campus layout.

Needs:
- Locate buildings.
- View simple directions.
- Find parking and university offices.

---

### 5. New Students

New students require assistance navigating the campus during orientation and their first semester.

Needs:
- Locate lecture halls.
- Find registration offices.
- Save favourite locations.
