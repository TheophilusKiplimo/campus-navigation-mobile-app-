# User Personas

## Persona 1: First-Year Student

### Profile

**Name:** Brian Kiptoo

**Age:** 19 Years

**Occupation:** First-Year University Student

### Background

Brian recently joined the university and is unfamiliar with the campus layout. He often gets lost while trying to find lecture halls, laboratories, and administrative offices.

### Goals

- Locate lecture halls quickly.
- Find university services.
- Save frequently visited locations.
- Reduce time spent asking for directions.

### Frustrations

- Difficult to understand campus maps.
- Lecture halls are poorly labelled.
- Arriving late for classes.
