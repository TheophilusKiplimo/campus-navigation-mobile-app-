# User Research Findings

## Participants

Five university users participated in the study.

- Three students
- One lecturer
- One visitor

---

## Summary of Findings

### Difficulty Finding Buildings

Four participants reported experiencing difficulties locating lecture halls, offices, or laboratories, particularly during their first weeks on campus.

### Current Navigation Methods

Participants currently rely on:

- Asking other students
- Campus security officers
- Printed notice boards
- Friends

### Desired Features

The most requested features were:

- Building search
- Navigation directions
- Lecture hall finder
- University service locator
- Saved favourite locations

### Conclusion

The findings indicate a clear need for a mobile application that provides quick and accurate campus navigation while remaining simple and easy to use.
